Everyday sustainability

Good habits make less waste

A few practical ways to reduce waste before it reaches the street or landfill.

↻

Choose reusable first

Carry a refillable bottle or bag, and repair or donate usable items instead of throwing them away.

♻

Recycle with care

Keep recyclables clean and dry, and check local guidance so non-recyclable items don't contaminate a whole load.

✿

Compost food scraps

Where a local service is available, separate suitable food and garden waste from general rubbish.

⌁

Handle special waste safely

Take batteries, electronics, chemicals and other hazardous items to an approved collection point.

Collection rules and drop-off services vary by location. Check your local authority's guidance before sorting or disposing of waste.
